02 — MANIFESTO

WHAT WE BELIEVE ABOUT
THE SURFACE OF THINGS

Digital is not a page.
It is a place you can feel.

We design for the moment before understanding — the half-second where a screen has not yet explained itself and the body already knows whether to stay.

Composition over components. Weight over decoration. Motion that means something: a camera move, not a bounce. Every surface is measured, every pause is authored, and nothing is centred by accident.

We build with type as architecture, light as material, and scroll as a timeline the visitor conducts with their thumb.
